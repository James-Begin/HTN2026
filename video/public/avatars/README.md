# Flyby avatar sources

These small, locally bundled images make the render reproducible and give the re-created public-post cards recognizable account identities. They are illustrative profile images, not claims about the image each account used when its featured post was published.

- `kanyewest.jpg`: [David Shankbone's portrait of Kanye West](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kanye_West_at_the_2009_Tribeca_Film_Festival_(crop_2).jpg), public domain. This copy was resized and recompressed.
- `elonmusk.jpg`: [Gage Skidmore's portrait of Elon Musk](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Elon_Musk_-_54820081119_(cropped).jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This copy was resized and recompressed; attribution to Gage Skidmore and the linked license applies to this image.
- All other `.jpg` files: public account profile images retrieved from `https://unavatar.io/twitter/<handle>` on September 30, 2026, then resized and recompressed. Account handles are the filenames. These images remain the property of their respective owners and appear here to identify the accounts in a documentary-style product demo.

The stylized X mark in `FlybyScene.tsx` identifies the platform and is not a claim of affiliation.
